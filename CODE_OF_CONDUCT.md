# Code of Conduct

This repository welcomes constructive contributions from people with different backgrounds and levels of experience.

## Our expectations

- Be respectful, patient and considerate in issues, pull requests and reviews.
- Focus feedback on the work and explain technical concerns clearly.
- Welcome questions and help others learn without belittling them.
- Do not harass, threaten, discriminate against, or personally attack other contributors.
- Do not publish anyone's private information without permission.

These expectations apply when participating in this repository or representing its community elsewhere.

## Reporting conduct concerns

Send concerns privately to [tjthavarshan@gmail.com](mailto:tjthavarshan@gmail.com). Include relevant links and context, but do not share sensitive information publicly. Reports will be reviewed with care and discretion; the maintainer may edit or remove inappropriate content or restrict participation when warranted.

## For projects reusing this file

Adapt the contact and enforcement process to your actual maintainers and team before copying it. A community policy should describe a process you can realistically support.

This concise repository policy is not a substitute for legal advice or a broader organization's code of conduct.
